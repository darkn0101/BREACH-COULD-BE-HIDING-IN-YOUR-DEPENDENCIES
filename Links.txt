NOTES

1. Public PoC repositories are untrusted code. Review the source before running anything.
2. Test only on systems you own or are explicitly authorized to assess, preferably in an isolated VM/lab.
3. "Not independently verified" means a working PoC was not confirmed during the research; it does not prove none exists.
4. The supplied identifier "GHSA-673q-mjm-r5xr" appears to be a typo; the identifier listed above is GHSA-673q-mjm6-r5xr.
5. The supplied identifier "GSHA-p3m5-59mp-jv5r" appears to have a typo in the prefix; the standard prefix is GHSA.
